🚀 ErpBuddy Interactive Demo

Complete Business Flow: Purchase → Sales → Profit → Reports

Welcome to the ErpBuddy Demo

This guided walkthrough will show you how ErpBuddy handles complete business transactions: from purchasing inventory through creating sales and seeing profits in real time.

Follow each step below to understand the entire flow. It takes about 5-10 minutes.

📦 Demo Scenario

Product: Super Basmati Rice (Premium Grade)

Purchase: 100 units @ ₹100/unit = ₹10,000 total cost

Sale: 20 units @ ₹120/unit = ₹2,400 revenue

Expected Profit: ₹2,400 - ₹2,000 = ₹400

1

Create Purchase Order

The first step is purchasing inventory. You'll create a purchase order for 100 units of Super Basmati Rice at ₹100 per unit. This establishes your inventory base and records the purchase in the system.

📌 Key Point: When you post the purchase, stock increases by 100 units and an Accounts Payable entry is created in the general ledger.
Sample Data:
Customer: Supplier XYZ
Date: Today
Item: Super Basmati Rice
Qty: 100 | Unit Price: ₹100 | Total: ₹10,000
2

Check Inventory Balance

After posting the purchase, check the inventory system to verify stock was received. You should see 100 units of Super Basmati Rice available.

🔍 What to Look For: Stock Balance should show: 100 units available, valued at ₹100/unit (FIFO costing)
Expected Inventory Entry:
Item: Super Basmati Rice
Available Qty: 100
Cost per Unit: ₹100 (FIFO)
Total Value: ₹10,000
3

Create Sales Invoice

Now create a sales invoice to a customer. Sell 20 units of Super Basmati Rice at ₹120 per unit. The system will automatically calculate profit based on the purchase cost.

⚡ Automatic Features:
• Stock validation: Ensures 20 units are available (they are!)
• Cost calculation: Uses FIFO method to determine cost = 20 × ₹100 = ₹2,000
• Profit calculation: Revenue ₹2,400 - Cost ₹2,000 = ₹400
Sample Data:
Customer: Customer ABC
Date: Today
Item: Super Basmati Rice
Qty: 20 | Unit Price: ₹120 | Total: ₹2,400
4

See Profit Calculation

When you submit the sales invoice, the system immediately shows you the profit breakdown. This is calculated from:

📊 Profit Breakdown:
Revenue (Selling Price): ₹2,400
Cost of Goods (FIFO): ₹2,000
──────────────────
Profit: ₹400
System Calculations:
• Revenue = 20 units × ₹120/unit = ₹2,400
• Cost = 20 units × ₹100/unit = ₹2,000 (FIFO costing)
• Profit = ₹2,400 - ₹2,000 = ₹400 profit margin on sale
5

View Reports & Ledger

All transactions are automatically recorded in the General Ledger. View reports to see the complete financial picture:

📋 Journal Entries Created:
• Purchase Journal: Credit A/P ₹10,000 | Debit Inventory ₹10,000
• Sale Revenue: Debit A/R ₹2,400 | Credit Sales ₹2,400
• Sale COGS: Debit COGS ₹2,000 | Credit Inventory ₹2,000
Final State:
• Inventory: 100 - 20 = 80 units remaining
• GL: All debits = all credits (balanced)
• P&L: Sales ₹2,400 - COGS ₹2,000 = Profit ₹400
